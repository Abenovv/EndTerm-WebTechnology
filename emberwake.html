Emberwake

An adventure game idea about exploring an old valley and discovering what happened there.

Original concept artwork, not a gameplay screenshot.

About the game

You play as a traveler carrying a small flame. The valley is full of abandoned buildings, old bridges, and hidden routes.

The flame helps you open paths and discover memories. The goal is to explore the valley and piece together its history.

We want Emberwake to focus on observation and simple puzzles rather than fast combat.
